# Prompt 01 — Channel Name

## Goal

Generate a memorable, futuristic and premium brand name for a technology-focused YouTube channel.

The name should:

- Be short and easy to remember.
- Sound modern and futuristic.
- Be suitable for AI, Robotics and Technology.
- Be unique enough for branding.
- Work well as a logo.
- Be easy to pronounce internationally.

---

## Prompt

Create 20 premium brand names for an English YouTube channel about Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Programming and Future Technology.

Requirements:

- One word only
- 5–8 letters preferred
- Easy to pronounce
- Premium sounding
- Available for branding if possible
- No numbers
- No special characters
- Avoid generic AI names

For every suggestion give a short explanation of why it works.

---

## Final Choice

NEXORA

Reason:

- Short
- Modern
- Memorable
- Strong visual identity
- Great logo potential
- Fits AI and technology branding
