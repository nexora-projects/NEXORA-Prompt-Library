# Prompt 02 — Brand Identity

## Goal

Create a complete visual identity for a modern AI and technology brand.

The identity should feel:

- Futuristic
- Clean
- Premium
- Minimal
- High-tech
- Trustworthy

---

## Prompt

Create a complete visual identity for a technology YouTube brand called NEXORA.

Requirements:

- Black and electric blue color palette
- Premium minimalist style
- Strong geometric shapes
- Suitable for YouTube branding
- Consistent across logo, banner and thumbnails
- Modern typography
- Clean negative space
- High contrast

The brand should represent:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Robotics
- Programming
- Innovation
- Future Technology

Do not use overcomplicated effects or unnecessary details.

---

## Final Direction

Brand Name:
NEXORA

Primary Colors:

- Black
- White
- Electric Blue

Style:

- Futuristic
- Minimal
- Premium

Brand Personality:

- Innovative
- Professional
- Modern
- Intelligent

Target Audience:

People interested in AI, Robotics, Programming and Future Technology.
