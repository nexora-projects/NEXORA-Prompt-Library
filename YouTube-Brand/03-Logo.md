# Prompt 03 — Logo Design

## Goal

Design a premium logo for the NEXORA brand.

---

## Prompt

Create a futuristic minimalist logo for a technology brand called NEXORA.

Requirements:

- Lettermark logo
- Black background
- White and electric blue
- Premium style
- Modern
- Minimal
- Scalable
- High contrast
- Easy to recognize

The logo should represent:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Robotics
- Programming
- Innovation

---

## Final Result

The final logo uses:

- Black background
- White primary shape
- Electric blue accent
- Minimal geometric design

Designed for:

- YouTube Profile
- GitHub
- Social Media
- Website

---

## Notes

A great logo should remain recognizable even at very small sizes.
